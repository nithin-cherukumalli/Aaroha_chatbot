Question: What is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)?
Answer: RAG retrieves relevant approved source material before generation so the answer can be grounded in that evidence and cite it. It improves traceability but does not guarantee correctness.

Question: Why do chunks overlap?
Answer: A small overlap helps preserve meaning that crosses a chunk boundary. Overlap should remain smaller than the target chunk size to avoid excessive duplication.

Question: What do embeddings do in retrieval?
Answer: Embeddings represent text in a vector space so semantically related questions and passages can be compared. Equal vector dimensions do not prove that two embedding models are compatible.

Question: What is a context window?
Answer: A context window is the amount of input and output text a language model can process in one request. Prompt instructions, the question, source passages, and the reserved output all consume this budget.
